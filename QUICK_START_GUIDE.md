## Day 6-7: Conduct Calls & Close (1 hour/call)

### Pre-Call Prep (15 min before each call)

1. **Review their demo submission** again
2. **Check their social media** (Instagram/Facebook) - understand them
3. **Prepare their "preview dashboard":**
   - Open Looker Studio (if you set it up)
   - Or create simple Google Sheet showing what they'd track
4. **Set intention:** Help them, don't sell them

### The Call Structure (15 minutes)

**Minute 0-2: Build Rapport**
```
"Hey [Name]! Thanks for jumping on. How's your day going?"

[Small talk]

"So you submitted your [meal] for analysis. Have you had a chance
to try that [tip you gave]?"
```

**Minute 2-7: Understand Their Situation**
```
"Tell me about your nutrition goals. What made you interested
in getting help?"

[Listen. Ask follow-ups:]
- "How long have you been working on this?"
- "What have you tried so far?"
- "What's the biggest challenge you're facing?"
- "What would success look like for you in 3 months?"
```

**Minute 7-12: Present Solution**
```
"Based on what you've shared, here's how I work with clients..."

[Share screen - show Looker dashboard or sample sheet]

"You'd track all your meals like the one you submitted. I review
them weekly and give you feedback like I did, but for every meal.

We'd also have weekly check-ins to adjust your plan based on what's
working.

Most clients see [specific result relevant to their goal] within 8-12 weeks."
```

**Minute 12-14: Pricing & Close**
```
"I have two options:

Option 1: $250/month - Unlimited meal tracking + weekly feedback + bi-weekly calls
Option 2: $400/month - Everything above + custom workout programming

Which one sounds like a better fit for where you're at?"

[Pause. Let them answer.]
```

**Minute 14-15: Handle Objections or Next Steps**

**If they say yes:**
```
"Awesome! I'll send you an onboarding email right after this call with:
- Payment link
- How to get started
- Your first meal plan

We can start as soon as Monday. Sound good?"
```

**If they hesitate:**
```
"What questions do you have?"

Common objections:
- "I need to think about it" → "Totally fair. What specifically do you need to think about?"
- "That's expensive" → "I get it. What were you expecting price-wise?" → Compare to eating out 1x/week
- "I'm not sure I can commit" → "That's why we do month-to-month. Try it for 30 days, if it's not working, no hard feelings."
```

**If they say no:**
```
"No problem at all. I appreciate you taking the time to chat!

If anything changes in the next few months, feel free to reach out.

And if you have quick nutrition questions along the way, I'm always
happy to answer via email. Seriously - no strings attached."

[They'll remember this and might come back later or refer friends]
```

## Troubleshooting

### "I posted but got zero submissions"

**Check:**
- Did you include clear CTA? ("Link in bio" or "DM me MEAL")
- Did you post at the right time? (Instagram: 11am, 2pm, 7pm)
- Did you engage with others' posts? (Algorithm rewards engagement)
- Is your offer compelling? (Emphasize "FREE" and "No catch")

**Try:**
- Post in Facebook groups (more direct access)
- DM warm leads directly
- Collaborate with other coaches (share each other's forms)

### "I got submissions but they're not in Lead Pool"

**Check:**
- Does your form response tab name include "lead"?
- Has the hourly sync run? (Check Lead Pool last modified time)
- Are the column headers matching the label synonyms?

**Fix:**
- Rename tab to include "lead"
- Run manual sync: Coach Actions → Run Full Sync
- Check LEAD_MANAGEMENT_GUIDE.md troubleshooting

### "I did calls but nobody converted"

**Reflect:**
- Did you listen more than you talked? (Should be 60% listening, 40% talking)
- Did you understand their REAL goal? (Not what they said, what they meant)
- Did you show them what they'd get? (Share screen with dashboard)
- Did you ask for the sale? (Must explicitly ask "Which option works for you?")

**Improve:**
- Record your next call (with permission)
- Listen back - where did you lose them?
- Ask friends to do mock calls with you
- Lower price temporarily to build confidence

### "I got 1 client but they're not engaging"

**Check:**
- Did you send onboarding email with clear next steps?
- Did you schedule their first check-in call?
- Are you responding to their meal submissions within 48 hours?

**Fix:**
- Send: "Hey! Haven't seen any meals from you yet. Need help getting started?"
- Offer 15-min onboarding call to walk them through
- Set expectations: "I need 3+ meal submissions per week to give you good feedback"
