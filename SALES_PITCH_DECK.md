# Presentation Notes (For Live Pitches)

### Delivery Tips:

**Pace:**
- Slides 1-3: Slow (build the pain)
- Slides 4-6: Medium (show the solution)
- Slides 7-8: Pause for questions (pricing)
- Slides 9-12: Fast (handle objections)
- Slides 13-15: Medium (add value)
- Slides 16-18: Slow (emotional close)

**Tone:**
- Empathetic (you understand their struggle)
- Confident (you know this works)
- Conversational (not salesy)

**Engagement:**
- Ask questions: "How many of you are getting <5 clients/month right now?"
- Poll: "Who's tried cold DMs? How'd that go?" *wait for groans*
- Interactive: "What would 5 new clients be worth to your business?"

**Objection Handling:**
- Welcome objections: "Great question!"
- Validate: "I'd be skeptical too"
- Reframe: "Here's what I've seen work..."
